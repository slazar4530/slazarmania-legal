SlazarMania - Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

This policy explains what the mobile game SlazarMania ("the game", "we") collects, why, who handles it, and how you can have it deleted. It applies to the game on Android, distributed through Google Play.

What the game collects

  • A player ID. You sign in with your Google Play Games account. Google Play Games gives the game a one-time code, which Unity Authentication exchanges for a player ID of the game. The game does not read your email address, your contacts or your Google account's other data.
  • Your game progress. Your hero's level, gear, skills, quests and the rest of your save are kept on the phone and a copy in the cloud, so that your hero is not lost when you change or reset your phone.
  • Your hero's name and build. The name you type for your hero, and a copy of your hero as she stands (level, gear, skills), are used in the arena and the Monster Tower. Other players can see your hero's name, her build, her rating and her place in the standings, and can fight against a copy of her.
  • Your messages. What you write in the game's chat (such as a guild's chat) is stored and shown to the players it is meant for. Players can block and report each other; a reported message and the player who wrote it may be reviewed, and messages that break the rules removed.
  • Your purchases. When you buy something in the game, Google Play takes the payment. The game receives and keeps a record of what you bought, so that you get it and keep it; it never sees your payment details.
  • Ads. The game shows ads from third-party advertising networks. To show and measure ads, these networks collect your device's advertising ID, information about your device, an approximate location worked out from your IP address, and how you interact with the ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics. Information about crashes and how the game runs on your device, so that problems can be found and fixed.

The game does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, payment details or your Google account's email address.

Why it is collected

  • To sign you in and keep your hero in your account (account management).
  • To save your progress and bring it back on any phone you sign in on (app functionality).
  • To run the arena, the Monster Tower and their standings, and to check that results follow the game's rules (app functionality and fair play).
  • To deliver what you buy and keep a record of it (app functionality).
  • To run the chat and keep it safe: blocking, reports and moderation (app functionality, safety).
  • To show ads and measure them (advertising, by the advertising networks).
  • To find and fix crashes (analytics and diagnostics).

We do not sell your data.

Who handles it

  • Google (Google Play Games Services) signs you in. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code and Leaderboards) stores your player ID, your save and your hero's copy, and runs the arena and the Tower, on our behalf. See Unity's privacy policy.
  • Google Play (Google Play Billing) takes the payments for purchases.
  • Advertising networks show the ads and collect what is described above under their own privacy policies. The networks the game uses are listed here, with links to their policies, from the version that shows ads.

Apart from what the advertising networks collect to show ads, we do not share your data with anyone else. Everything sent between the game and these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

How long it is kept

Your player ID, save, hero's copy, purchase records and messages are kept for as long as your account exists. Reports are kept as long as they are needed to deal with them. The copy on your phone is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Deleting your account and data

You can ask for your account and all data kept for it to be deleted at any time. Send an email to slazar4530@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my SlazarMania account" and your hero's name. We delete your player ID, your save, your hero's copy, your messages and your entries in the standings within 30 days, and answer you when it is done. A record of purchases may be kept as long as the law requires. Uninstalling the game alone does not delete the copy in the cloud.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
